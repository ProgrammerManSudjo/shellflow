Put the Rainmeter widget (the cookie clock skin) here, as its own folder, exactly as it is in
  C:\Users\<you>\Documents\Rainmeter\Skins\<SkinName>
so this repo has  rainmeter\<SkinName>\...  (its .ini files, @Resources and so on).

The installer then offers to copy every folder in here to  Documents\Rainmeter\Skins.  Rainmeter itself is installed separately: https://www.rainmeter.net
Before you upload: remove anything personal from the .ini files (paths with your user name, API keys, your city), and check that you have the right to share the skin and its fonts and images.
